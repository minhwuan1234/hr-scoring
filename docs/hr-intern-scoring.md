# HR Intern experience scoring — agreed rules

Position: Talent Acquisition & Employer Branding Intern. Scope: experience only. The current frontend is a clearly labeled UI preview; these rules are a contract for the scoring service, not an implemented model or keyword engine.

## Dimensions

| Dimension | Maximum |
| --- | ---: |
| Learning drive / Ham học và khả năng apply | 30 |
| Learning agility / Học nhanh | 20 |
| Long-term commitment và direction phù hợp | 20 |
| Khả năng hỗ trợ tuyển dụng và điều phối quy trình hiring | 8 |
| Tổ chức dữ liệu, trackers, templates, hồ sơ và độ cẩn thận trong execution | 7 |
| Hỗ trợ employer branding / content execution cho tuyển dụng | 5 |
| Kỷ luật quy trình: checklist, SOP, workflow, phát hiện thiếu sót | 4 |
| Mức độ phù hợp về học vấn, định hướng nghề, và hứng thú với talent / culture / employer branding | 3 |
| Công cụ nền tảng, English cơ bản, và khả năng dùng AI hỗ trợ | 3 |

Potential / learning trajectory totals 70; functional readiness totals 30. A prior HR internship alone must not dominate. Learning requires evidence of application, not just courses or certificates. Progression, increased responsibility, and a coherent activity trajectory can support an inference of learning agility. Short activities alone do not imply weak commitment. Academic achievements may support learning capacity; a school name alone is insufficient.

## Keyword ownership

- Models propose keywords and phrases with CV evidence and a suggested dimension.
- New keyword mappings require human approval before scoring.
- An exact word or phrase can contribute only once per CV and to one dimension.
- Repeated appearances of that same phrase add no additional points.
- Synonymous words and phrases remain distinct and can each count. Do not merge them automatically.
- Human decisions become reference mappings/examples for subsequent model runs; this is not automatic model fine-tuning.
- Each dimension is capped; the total is capped at 100.

## Visible output

Only the total score and the nine dimension scores with their maximums are shown. Do not display model internals, prompts, keyword approval mechanics, or reasoning on the scoring screen. Preserve an explicit illustrative-result disclosure until real scoring is connected.
